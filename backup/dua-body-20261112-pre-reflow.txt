HASBUNALLAHU WA NIMAL WAKIL

ya-Hayum ya-Gayum by Your mercy I call on You to set right all my affairs. Do not place me in charge of my soul even for the blinking of an eye (i.e. a moment).

O Allah! Please arrange my affairs in the best way possible, in Your way, and make it easy for me ya Rabbim.

And please provide for me

through Your strength, through Your

protection, through Your wisdom, through

Your

knowledge, through Your guidance, through

Your patience, through Your courage and through Your mercy. Through Your bounty and through Your help. And through Your peace and safety and sustenance.

And please protect me from Sheytan, his ploys, his whispers, his followers, the wrongdoing people, the people you have led astray, those with sickness in their heart and nazar. And please protect me from the hypocrites, the disbelievers, those in falsehood, and those you are displeased with. And from my own nafs ya Rabbim.

A'udhu Billahi Min ash-Shaytan ir-Rajim

O Allah! Please incline those good doers, the

righteous the virtuous. Those you have guided

and those you have turned to with Mercy. And those you are pleased with, to me and me to them.

O Allah! Please forgive me for my short comings and my wrongdoings. O Allah! Please forgive me if I make a mistake or I forget. And by Your Mercy and Grace, make me pleasing to You.
